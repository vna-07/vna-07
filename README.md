*Small Enterprise Early-Warning & Response: "Paper in. Decisions out."*

### 🧾 StreetCred
**Verified ledger + credit scoring for informal traders**

## 🏗️ Spotlight: How SEER Works

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A["📷 Photo of handwritten ledger"] --> B["Pass 1: Gemini Vision<br/>structure & transcribe"]
    B --> C["Pass 2: Gemini Vision<br/>verify & reconcile"]
    C --> D["⚙️ Deterministic TS engine<br/>stockout + credit risk maths"]
    D --> E["📝 Drafted actions<br/>supplier orders · customer reminders"]
    E --> F{"👤 Owner approval"}
    F -- approve --> G["📲 Telegram / Email send"]
    F -- reject --> H["🗑️ Discarded"]
    D --> I["💬 SERA analyst<br/>ask questions of your own data"]
```

**Design principles:** the LLM *reads and writes*, but never *calculates*. All numbers come from a deterministic engine. A human is always in the loop. Fallback models keep it alive when one provider doesn't.
